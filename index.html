01 — Who I Am

About Me

I'm a GPU Systems Engineer with 2 years of combined industry and research experience building high-performance compute pipelines. My work sits at the intersection of CUDA kernel engineering, GPU microarchitecture, and production ML systems.

At Siemens Healthineers, I built quantitative PET-CT clinical workflows and engineered CUDA kernels that fused 10 sequential filtering stages into a single kernel pass — cutting global memory traffic by ~10× on real medical imaging data. I care deeply about the hardware-software boundary: roofline analysis, occupancy tuning, latency hiding, and memory coalescing are how I think.

  • HPC parallelism — MPI domain decomposition, OpenMP, stream concurrency
